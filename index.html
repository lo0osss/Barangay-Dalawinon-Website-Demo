Your council

Barangay and SK officials

The Barangay Council is composed of the Punong Barangay, seven Sangguniang Barangay Members, the SK Chairperson as Ex-Officio Member, the Barangay Secretary, and the Barangay Treasurer, under the Local Government Code of 1991. The Sangguniang Kabataan is composed of the SK Chairperson, seven SK Kagawad, the SK Secretary, and the SK Treasurer, under the SK Reform Act of 2015.

Barangay Council

Sangguniang Kabataan Council

Mandate of the Sangguniang Kabataan

Under Republic Act No. 10742, as amended by Republic Act No. 11768, the SK represents the youth in local governance. It identifies the needs and concerns of young residents, formulates the Comprehensive Barangay Youth Development Plan (CBYDP) and the Annual Barangay Youth Investment Program (ABYIP), approves its annual budget, promulgates resolutions, and implements programs on education, environment, disaster risk reduction, employment and livelihood, health, anti-drug abuse, gender sensitivity, sports, and leadership training.

Developer mode

Signed in as BACKEND_CODER. You hold every permission on the site, and you can rewrite any text on any page directly.

Switch on Edit page text in the top bar, then click a heading, paragraph or label and type over it. Changes save when you click away and persist in this browser. Lists that the site builds automatically — announcements, programs, records, officials — are edited through their own panels instead.

Move and remove

While signed in as BACKEND_CODER, small ▲ ▼ buttons appear beside records, archived files, announcements, programs and officials — use them to reorder any list. A red ✕ on an official card removes that person, and Delete buttons appear on every table. Order and removals are kept on this device.

Pages shown in the menu

Resident submissions

Everything sent by residents through the feedback form — inquiries, suggestions, document requests, concerns and anything else. Each one is assigned to a responsible officer and closed once it is answered, released, or the reason for withholding it is explained.

Designated volunteer youth leaders

One volunteer leader for each of the six puroks. They relay announcements to residents with limited internet access and help organise local activities, so the site never becomes the only way information travels.
